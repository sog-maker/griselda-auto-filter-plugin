# Changelog

## 2.2.3

The first public release.

- Interface in React, drawn by Microsoft WebView2 inside the plug-in window, fixed at 704 × 461 points
- Filter: low-pass and high-pass with resonance and mix; slope 12, 24 or 48 dB as an automation parameter
- LFO with five shapes and four targets, tempo sync and an envelope follower as automation parameters
- Distortion: soft clip, hard clip, tube and foldback, with amount, tone and mix
- Pitch shifter with semitones, cents and mix
- Grain time stretch with two overlapping, Hann-windowed grains
- Three-band EQ
- Spectral field and level meter
- Five factory presets; export and import of all settings as XML
- VST3 and standalone for Windows x64
